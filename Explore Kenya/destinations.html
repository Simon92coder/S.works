Destinations

Seven places that show Kenya's range, from savanna to coast to snow-capped peaks.

Maasai Mara

Best time: July to October

Game drives, balloon flights and Maasai village visits.

Amboseli National Park

Best time: June to October

Large elephant herds and clear views of Kilimanjaro.

Diani Beach

Best time: December to March

Snorkelling, dhow trips and quiet palm-lined sand.

Lamu Old Town

Best time: January to March

A car-free Swahili island with centuries of history.

Mount Kenya

Best time: January to February

Guided treks through forest, moorland and glaciers.

Lake Nakuru

Best time: Year-round

Flamingos, rhinos and easy day trips from Nairobi.
